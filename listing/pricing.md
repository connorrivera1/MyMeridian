# MyMeridian App Store pricing details

Paste this content only into Shopify's designated **Pricing details** field.
Do not copy the prices into the app icon, feature media, screenshots,
introduction, feature bullets, or app-details field.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Included monthly orders | Additional order usage |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Starter | $49 USD | $490 USD | 1,000 | $0.05 USD per order above 1,000, capped at $50 USD per billing period |
| Growth | $129 USD | $1,290 USD | 5,000 | $0.05 USD per order above 5,000, capped at $50 USD per billing period |
| Scale | $299 USD | $2,990 USD | 20,000 | $0.05 USD per order above 20,000, capped at $50 USD per billing period |

Every plan begins with a 14-day free trial. Monthly subscriptions renew every
30 days; annual subscriptions renew every year. Annual billing is one payment
and is priced at ten times the corresponding monthly rate. Taxes charged by
Shopify are additional where applicable.

Charges and usage records are created only through Shopify Billing and appear
on the merchant's Shopify invoice. Merchants can upgrade or downgrade in the
app without contacting support. Upgrades follow Shopify's approval flow;
downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

An invite-only, email-bound founding offer may discount an eligible initial
monthly subscription by 15% for its first 12 billing intervals. It is not a
public code, does not apply to annual billing, and must be described in the
reviewer instructions only when the supplied reviewer account is eligible.
