## Reviewer guardrails

- Each screenshot is a distinct MyMeridian application surface rendered from
  the seeded review store; no browser chrome, customer-identifying data, or
  non-MyMeridian product branding appears.
- The imagery labels the store data as seeded demo data. It must never be
  described in the listing as customer results, measured merchant outcomes, or
  an independently verified performance claim.
- The media contains no MyMeridian subscription price, discount, free-trial
  offer, or other app-billing information. Paste `pricing.md` only into
  Shopify's designated Pricing details field.
- Do not upload `screenshots-held/`; it is retained only as provenance for
  replaced candidates.
