Straight Answers

Questions before
you connect your store.

The details that matter, without the sales pitch.

Where Do The Product Costs Come From?

Shopify’s variant cost field supplies COGS. You set shipping, fees, pick-and-pack and overhead rules; missing costs are marked incomplete.

Does This Include My Ad Spend?

Yes, after a Meta, Google or TikTok source is connected and synced. Until then, profit is shown before paid marketing.

How Much History Will I See On Day One?

The first sync includes 60 days of Shopify orders. More history requires Shopify approval.

Can It Change Anything In My Store?

No. MyMeridian has read-only access and cannot edit prices, products or orders.

When Do Pricing Recommendations Show Up?

After MyMeridian sees a price change following install. It waits for that history rather than guessing, and changes are capped at 25%.

How Does Billing Work?

Monthly and annual plans are billed through Shopify. Eligible merchants receive any launch benefit through their verified waitlist account when an offer is available.
