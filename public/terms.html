These terms govern use of MyMeridian, a profitability dashboard for Shopify stores. They apply when a merchant installs or uses the service. MyMeridian is not publicly distributed yet; its final individual publisher identity and governing law will be supplied before Shopify submission.

1. The Service

MyMeridian reads a merchant’s Shopify orders, products, cost per item and fulfilments, applies the cost rules that merchant configures, and presents profit, product, pricing and fulfilment analysis derived from them. It identifies a missing, measured or configured input instead of silently turning an unavailable cost into zero. It requests read-only Shopify access and makes no change to any store.

The service is provided as it exists at the time of use. Features may be added, changed or withdrawn; where a change removes something a paid plan was sold on, affected merchants will be told before it takes effect.

2. Eligibility and Accounts

MyMeridian is available to merchants operating a Shopify store. The Shopify installation path requires no separate signup: access is authenticated by Shopify, and whoever Shopify authorises as a staff member of the store with app access can use it. Keeping that staff list correct is the merchant’s responsibility. Optional standalone MyMeridian accounts, where enabled, use their own authentication, multi-factor protection and sensitive-action reauthentication.

7. Analysis Is Information, Not Advice

This clause is the one worth reading twice. MyMeridian produces profit figures, pricing recommendations and capacity forecasts from a store’s own data and the cost rules a merchant enters. Those outputs are information to inform a decision, not professional advice, and they are not accounting, tax, legal or investment advice.

  • Accuracy depends on inputs we do not control — above all cost per item. Where a cost is missing, MyMeridian marks the input as missing and qualifies the resulting figure rather than presenting the missing amount as a measured zero.
  • Pricing recommendations are fitted only to price history MyMeridian observes after installation, and are capped and confidence-gated. They remain predictions; demand may not respond as modelled, and acting on one is the merchant’s commercial decision.
  • Figures shown are not a substitute for the merchant’s accounts, and should not be filed or relied on as accounting records.
