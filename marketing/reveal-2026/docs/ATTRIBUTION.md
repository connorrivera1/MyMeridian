## Music bed
- **Track:** "Cinematic Technology" — SoundGalleryByDmitryTaras
- **Source:** Pixabay, https://pixabay.com/music/orchestral-cinematic-technology-581404/
- **File:** `raw/audio/cinematic-technology-581404.mp3` (2:00, 256 kbps MP3)
- **License:** Pixabay Content License — free for commercial use, no attribution
  required (attribution given anyway). Cannot be resold/redistributed as a
  standalone audio file. https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
- **Use in film:** window 57.7–117.7s of the track under the full 60s cut, with
  volume automation (restrained open → build → ducked under the closing brand
  tone). Selected deterministically: per-second RMS analysis of three
  candidates; this one had the only true quiet-development → arrival → rising
  arc. Runner-up candidates retained in `raw/audio/` under the same license.
- **Artlist note:** Artlist could not be checked this session (Chrome extension
  offline), so per the production rule the film uses a free-use track. To swap
  in an Artlist track later: drop the file in `raw/audio/`, adjust `MUSIC=` and
  the offset in `source/motion/mix.sh`, re-run `mix.sh` — picture is untouched.

## Sound design
- **File:** `source/render-cache-v2/sfx.wav`
- All effects are synthesized from first principles (sine/noise DSP in
  `source/motion/audio-synth.mjs`) — no sample packs, no AI-generated audio.
  Hits are generated from `markers.json`, which is exported from the film
  timeline itself, so every impact is sample-locked to picture. No license
  required; the stem is original work made for this film.

## Interface footage
Every frame of product UI is a direct 3840×2160 Chrome capture of the real
MyMeridian app (branch `feature/mymeridian-web-accounts`, seeded Northwind
Supply Co. demo store) or the real `site/index.html` landing page. No UI,
chart, number, or text was regenerated by an image/video model.
