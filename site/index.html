Method

Confidence is easy to print.
Being right is the product.

Three things MyMeridian will not do, however much better the dashboard would look if it did.

01

A missing cost never becomes a zero.

Where an input is absent, MyMeridian shows a dash and says which figure it affects. A blank is information; a zero is a lie that compounds through every total above it.

Ad spend$0.00 → Ad spend—no synced source
02

No product is called a loser on a guess.

A loss verdict requires a measured cost. Products with no recorded COGS are held out of the ranking and named as unclassified, so you never discontinue a line on the strength of a blank field.

Trail Runner 2Bleeding → Trail Runner 2Unclassifiedno cost recorded
03

Price history is observed, never invented.

Elasticity is fitted only to price changes MyMeridian watched happen after installation. It cannot reconstruct the years before it arrived, so it does not pretend to — it waits, and tells you it is waiting.

Suggested price$44.90 → Suggested priceNot yetneeds observed movement

The result is a number with a known shape: you can see what went into it, what did not, and exactly how far to trust it.

Straight answers

What you'd ask on a call.
Answered before you install.

Every answer here is the same one the app gives you on the screen it affects. None of them get better if you read them twice.

Where do the product costs come from?

Shopify's own cost-per-item field on each variant. Shipping, payment fees, pick-and-pack and overhead come from cost rules you set once. Where a variant has no cost recorded, MyMeridian says so and holds that product out of the ranking — it does not score it at zero and call it profitable.

Does this include my ad spend?

Growth and Scale stores can connect Meta Ads, Google Ads and TikTok Ads from inside MyMeridian. Until a connector is healthy and its spend has synced, the app labels profit as before paid marketing and shows unavailable spend rather than silently treating it as zero.

How much history will I see on day one?

Sixty days. That is the window Shopify's standard order access returns, so it is what the first sync can cover. MyMeridian puts that in a banner instead of drawing a chart that starts at your install date and letting you assume the store had no earlier trading. Longer history needs an extra approval from Shopify.

Can it change anything in my store?

No. MyMeridian asks for four read-only permissions — orders, products, fulfilments and inventory — and never requests write access to any of them. It cannot edit a price, a product or an order. That is a property of what it installed with, not a promise about how it behaves.

When do pricing recommendations show up?

Once MyMeridian has watched a price actually move. Elasticity is fitted only to changes observed after install, so the pricing view opens by telling you it is waiting rather than fitting a curve to years it never saw. Any move it does suggest is capped at 25%.

How does billing work?

At launch, monthly and annual plans will be billed through Shopify. Founding Merchant eligibility is for 15% off the first 12 months of a monthly plan; it is not automatically combined with annual pricing.
